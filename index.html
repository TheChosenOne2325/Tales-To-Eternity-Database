Tales To Eternity

Tales To Eternity is a tabletop RPG system that seeks a balance between complexity and simplicity, so that everything works as well as possible. It also standardizes many rules and mechanics to make future content easier to create.

It is a single, unified system: there will be no other editions, and everything is online, which makes updates easy. In the future, more books will be released with new rules, classes, races, and more.

The most important rule is to have fun.
