Versions

Tales To Eternity uses two numbers: X.Y.

  • X goes up when a new book is released (2.0, 3.0…).
  • Y goes up with corrections and adjustments (1.1, 1.2…).

1.0 — Inauguration

November 9, 2026

The first release of Tales To Eternity, with the three core books:

  • Fragments Of The Cosmos (FTC): the Player's Book.
  • Creation Of The Cosmos (COC): the Game Master's Book.
  • Rupture Of The Cosmos (ROC): the Monster Book.

Databases: Races, Classes, Specializations, Skills, Languages, Items, Spells, Bestiary, Monster Abilities, Actions, Conditions, and Damage Types.

Tools: Encounter Builder, and a Level and Role selector on every monster.
